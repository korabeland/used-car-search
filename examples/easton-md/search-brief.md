# Search brief (example: Easton, Maryland)

**Illustrative example.** This is the brief from a real search, kept to show what a filled-in brief looks like. It contains no live listings, dealers or findings. Copy what's useful into the root `search-brief.md`; don't edit this file for your own search.

## Where

- Home base (town or ZIP): Easton, MD 21601
- How far you'll drive to buy: about 60 miles, or one hour of normal driving
- What to do with dealers just past that line: ask me

## Budget

- Target price: $25,000 cash, before tax and fees
- Stretch threshold: $28,000 asking price
- Paying with: cash

## Which cars

- Makes, models and model years: 2018 or newer Toyota RAV4, Toyota Highlander, Honda CR-V
- Years, generations or engines to avoid: none beyond the year floor

## Must-haves

Always-on gates apply (matching VIN, clean title, no odometer/theft/structural issues, full history reviewed, independent inspection allowed). Added for this search:

- AWD
- Dark interior
- Reverse camera

## Strong preferences

- Preferred powertrain: hybrid
- Mileage you'd like to stay under: 100,000
- Owners: one owner preferred
- Service records: dense, documented service history preferred
- Anything else: none

## How the car will be used

- Miles per year: 10,000 to 15,000
- Climate and roads: winter driving on the Eastern Shore, daily commute
- Passengers and gear: (omitted from this example)
- Anything else that matters day to day: none

## Sellers

- Franchise dealers of the brands above: yes
- Other-brand franchise dealers: yes
- Independent dealers: yes, only if transparent (VINs, pricing, history access, inspection allowed)
- Private sellers: no

## Timing

- How soon you need the car: urgent
- How often listings should be rechecked: promising cars daily, dealer inventories every two to three days

## Scoring choices

- Price bands: defaults (15 points at or below $25,000; 11 up to $26,500; 6 up to $28,000; 0 above)
- Mileage bands: defaults
- Category weights: defaults

## If results are thin

1. Accept 100,000 to 115,000 miles with exceptional service history.
2. Give more weight to non-hybrid AWD examples.
3. Look harder at the over-$28,000 cars already found, without assuming a discount.
4. Widen the drive distance.
